Adjustments Builder

Build a student's reasonable adjustments — choose a functional domain and activity, set the expected level, tick what applies, then export a Word document, a spreadsheet or a printed copy.

Open the builder Download for offline use

Nothing is installed and no account is needed. The downloaded file works with no internet connection.

Read this before you use what it produces. The adjustment library is a working draft. It was assembled in one school and expanded with AI assistance. It is not departmental policy, not approved guidance, and not a substitute for professional judgement or your school's own processes. Every adjustment it writes is a starting point — someone who knows the student must check that it describes what will actually happen before it is agreed or acted on.

Your data stays with you

The app runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no sign-in and no network call of any kind. Student details never leave the device they are typed on, and nobody — including the author — can see them.

  • Work in progress is kept in that browser's local storage, on that device only.
  • Clearing your browser data clears your work. Use Save file to keep a copy you can reopen.
  • If your browser or school policy blocks site data, the app says so rather than pretending to save.

What's in it

  • 686 activity-level adjustments across 6 functional domains and 31 activities.
  • 93 role and coordination supports, covering leadership, wellbeing, health, curriculum and transition roles.
  • A setup guide that asks one question at a time, so a school can set its own words, roles, tiers, wording style and document layout before anyone builds a plan.
  • Exports a real .docx and .xlsx, or prints to PDF.

Made it yours

Every label is renameable: what you call a student, an adjustment, a level and a tier, and every staff role. Turn off the roles your school does not have and they disappear from the wording and every list. Add your own logo, colour and heading to the exported document.
